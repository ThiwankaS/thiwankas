Who I am

Hive Helsinki (42) graduate based in Helsinki. I treat engineering as an ongoing experiment: write, break, measure, fix, and repeat.

C/C++ on Linux is home base. From there, I stretch into Python, Qt, firmware, and TinyML — not as a checklist, but as disciplines I return to with hands on the keyboard.

How the pieces connect

  • Systems first Processes, memory, networking, concurrency: understand the machine before decorating it.
  • Tooling & interfaces Python for glue and prototyping; Qt when software needs a clean, reliable face.
  • Devices Firmware and board-level work, where constraints make every byte and clock cycle count.
  • Edge intelligence Pulling ML out of notebooks and squeezing it into real hardware limits.
  • Usable software Turning those deeper layers into tools people can actually run, touch, and use.

Each loop feeds the next: a systems bug teaches me about the board; a UI forces clearer APIs; an on-device constraint reshapes both model and code.

What keeps me going

I’m not optimising for a title. I’m optimising for depth — staying curious, shipping rough-then-refined iterations, and building a practice I can trust.
