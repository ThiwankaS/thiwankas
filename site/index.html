Who I am

I’m a Hive Helsinki (42) graduate, shaped by peer learning that rewards solving hard problems under real pressure and by earlier years in people-facing and operations roles that built how I work with others.

I take initiative, stay accountable, and aim for high integrity when the work gets messy or the path is unclear. Business context matters to me as much as the craft. I try to see the need, the people involved, and the practical outcome before I dig in.

What I care about most is behavior I can explain: understand the situation, reproduce the issue, then own the change myself. Tools and AI can speed research; they don’t replace my judgment or understanding.

What keeps me going

Curiosity keeps me moving. I stay close to questions that matter, teach myself what I need along the way, and only trust an idea once I’ve put it to work.
